India state and union territory boundary map

The map is derived from geoBoundaries India ADM1 geometry (36 administrative
areas), sourced from the DataMeet India community and Election Commission of
India. The source boundary data represents 2011 and was released by
geoBoundaries on December 12, 2023. The geometry was simplified and converted
to SVG for local, offline use.
